# Question bank: lifecycle

Generic phases and typical enabling systems and risks are in `assets/data/baseline-lifecycle.json`. Re-word for the user's sector before asking.

## Per phase
- Does this phase exist for your system? In your field, what is it called?
- What system or team makes this phase happen (the enabling system)? Does it already exist or must it be built too?
- Who is involved in this phase? Who pays for it?
- What must be true of the system for this phase to go well? What would make it painful?
- Does this phase overlap with another one? Which and how?
- What can go wrong here that we should capture now?

## Whole lifecycle
- Where does the system first meet the real world, and who is there?
- Who has to learn something to use or support it, and when?
- What gets upgraded or extended after launch, and how often?
- What happens at the end: removal, data or material recovery, disposal, handover?
- Is any phase done by someone outside your organization (a supplier, a partner, a customer)?

## Sector adaptation hints
- Software: production is build and packaging; deployment is release and rollout; operations is the live service; retirement is sunset and data migration.
- Hardware or physical products: production is manufacturing; deployment includes shipping and installation; retirement includes recycling.
- Services: training may be onboarding; development includes service design and piloting.
- Always check the user's own words before imposing these.

## Challenge prompts
- "You marked <phase> not applicable. If nobody does it, what happens when it is needed?"
- "In <phase>, who else do you see besides the ones listed?"
- "Does anything you need in <phase A> limit what you can do in <phase B>?"
