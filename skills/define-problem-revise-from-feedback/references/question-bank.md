# Question bank: feedback revision

## Ingesting
- Where did this come from (meeting, email, document comment) and when? Who said it?
- Is this one point or several? Can I split it?
- Which artifact items does it touch? Which IDs?

## Classifying
- Is it a question to answer, a change to something we wrote, a new need, a conflict with another stakeholder, or an approval or rejection?
- Does the wording change what the system must do, or only how we described it?

## Triage (give a recommendation for each)
- Accept: what changes and where? Does it affect other artifacts?
- Reject: what is the rationale and who tells the stakeholder?
- Defer: until when, and what would trigger it?
- Clarify: what exactly should we ask, and whom?

## Conflicts
- <Stakeholder A> wants X, <Stakeholder B> wants not X. Who decides? What would each give up? Is a trade-off requirement the right answer?

## Approvals
- Who is approving, and for what scope (the acceptance requirements, or everything)?
- Approved as is, or with conditions? What are they, and are they written down?
- If disapproved: which artifact items drive it, and which skill revisits them?

## Results of the feasibility plan
- Which questions are answered? What was the result against the decision criteria?
- Does any requirement now need to change?

## Challenge prompts
- "This 'clarification' changes the threshold in <REQ>. Is it a change request?"
- "Accepting this affects <artifact>. Do we re-confirm it now or flag it?"
- "The approval depends on <condition>. Where is that recorded?"
