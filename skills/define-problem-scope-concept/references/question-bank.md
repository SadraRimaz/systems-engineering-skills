# Question bank: scoping a concept

Pick the questions the user has not already answered. Rephrase in the user's domain. Offer a recommended answer for each.

## Problem and purpose
- What problem does this solve, and for whom? What do they do about it today?
- What goes wrong or costs too much about the current way?
- Why now? What changed that makes this worth doing?
- If this works perfectly, what larger purpose does it serve? (The larger purpose can change which problem is the right one.)
- What would you be unwilling to give up, whatever the design?

## Mission objectives and value
- What would the people who pay for this call a success? How would they measure it?
- What are the one to three objectives that matter most? What is traded off against what (cost, time, quality, risk)?
- In one sentence: why would someone choose this over doing nothing?

## Day in the life
- Walk me through one ordinary day or use, start to finish, from the user's point of view.
- What is the worst day: what goes wrong, and what must still work?

## System of interest
- What should we call the thing we are defining?
- Without saying how it works inside: what does it take in, and what does it give back?
- Is this one system, or a part of something larger that already exists?

## Boundary
- Who or what will clearly interact with it but is not part of it?
- What is certainly part of it? What is a judgment call?
- What exists already that it must work with or replace?
- What can affect it that it cannot affect (regulation, weather, market, calendar)?
- What do we not know yet about the boundary, and who can find out?

## Constraints, assumptions, non-goals
- What is fixed: budget, date, law, platform, existing commitments?
- What are we assuming is true that nobody has checked?
- What will this explicitly not do?

## Domain
- What sector or industry is this in? Which words do people there use for design, build, release, operate, support, improve, retire?
- Which of the generic lifecycle terms (see `assets/data/baseline-lifecycle.json`) mean something different or are not used in your field?

## Challenge prompts
- "You said <technology or product>. What need does it serve, and what else could meet it?"
- "Is <statement> the problem, or a symptom of a larger one?"
- "Could two people read <term> differently? What would each expect?"
