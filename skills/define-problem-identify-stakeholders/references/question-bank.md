# Question bank: stakeholders

Class prompts live in `assets/data/stakeholder-classes.json`. Re-word each for the sector, for example for software: "producer" becomes "who builds and packages releases", "deployer" becomes "who ships and rolls out".

## Opening
- Who is involved with this system, from the first idea to its end of life?
- Who will be happy or upset about it, even if they never touch it?

## Per class (ask a few at a time)
- Who pays for it? Is that the same party in every phase? Who decides to keep paying?
- Who designs and integrates it? Who needs requirements stable enough to work in parallel?
- Who builds or produces each unit? Under what constraints?
- Who checks that it works and is acceptable? What evidence do they need?
- Who delivers and installs it? Who trains the people who will use or support it?
- Who operates it day to day, and in what conditions?
- Who benefits directly from its outputs? Which user groups differ in needs, skill or ability?
- Who could be harmed or inconvenienced by it without choosing to use it?
- Who diagnoses and repairs it? Who keeps it supplied and supported?
- Who will change or extend it later? Who retires it and how?

## Depth
- Which user groups differ enough that their needs could conflict?
- Whose view do you currently weigh most? Whose do you hear least?
- Who regulates this, certifies it, or insures it?
- For large groups: who can speak for them, and how reliable is that?
- Who has veto power over launch or continuation?

## Evidence
- How do you know this stakeholder exists and cares? Have you spoken to them?
- What would they say they need, in their own words?

## Challenge prompts
- "You did not name anyone as <class>. Is nobody doing that, or is it someone you have not thought of?"
- "<Stakeholder> asked for <solution>. What need is behind it?"
- "Who pays at each stage, and does the payer in one stage care about the cost in another?"
