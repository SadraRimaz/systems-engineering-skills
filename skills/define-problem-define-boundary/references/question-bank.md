# Question bank: boundary

## Classifying
- Does the system send something to <actor>, receive something from it, or both?
- Is <actor> something the system acts on, or something that only affects the system?
- Is <actor> there to provide a service the system needs to be built, fielded or supported (enabling)?
- Does <actor> already exist (legacy)? Must the system conform to its interfaces?

## Interfaces
- Which of these exchanges are data, which energy, which matter?
- Is this a thing the system consumes or produces, or a rule it must obey (control)?
- Are two exchanges really the same interface used twice?

## Controls
- What laws, standards or policies apply in this phase? Who enforces them?
- Which limits come from the outside and cannot be negotiated?

## Boundary decisions
- Which of these functions could sit inside or outside the system? What changes in cost, schedule or risk for each choice?
- Who decides, and who pays if it moves?
- What will the stakeholders do if the boundary is drawn the other way?

## Context
- What do you have to plan for but cannot control: weather, demand, regulation, competitors, other organizations?
- Can the system be affected without affecting back? Which ones?

## Challenge prompts
- "<Actor> gets no output from the system in <phase>. Is it context, or is an exchange missing?"
- "If <function> moved outside the system, who would own its interface?"
- "You said <thing> is out of scope. Who is responsible for it, and have they agreed?"
